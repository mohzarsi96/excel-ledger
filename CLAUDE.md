# Excel Traders — Sales & Incentive Ledger

Single-file web app (`public/index.html`; plain HTML/JS, Chart.js + SheetJS from CDN, ExcelJS for the purchasing Excel exports), on Firebase:
Firestore (modular SDK 12.4.0) + email/password Auth + Hosting. Firebase project `excel-ledger-eea70`, site https://excel-ledger-eea70.web.app.
Hosting deploys from GitHub Actions on every push to `main` (`.github/workflows/`). Keep the repo private.
Copied from the Alfa/Muzaf ledger (a separate app on a separate Firebase project); keep the UI as is (60-30-10 blue/slate/amber theme).

## Data model (Firestore)
One company: Excel Traders (`excel`), listed in `COMPANIES` in public/index.html (more can be added there and in `scripts/set-access.mjs`; the sign-in screen then offers a picker and the sidebar a switcher). Company data lives under `companies/{companyId}/` (orders, reps, channels, meta); the paths below are relative to that. A company entry may set `bizSplit:true` to split dashboard pies and the Incentives filter Glass vs Lifting (channel SR = Lifting); Excel Traders doesn't, so they go by channel.
- `orders/{id}`: ref, channel, rep, ledgerMonth ('YYYY-MM'), gstRate, grossSales, totalCost (null if pending),
  paymentStatus ('none'|'full'|'noteligible'), incentiveMonth ('YYYY-MM' or null; set when carried over),
  incentiveLocked (bool), notes
- `reps/{code}` {code,name}; `channels/{code}` {code,name}
- `users/{uid}` (root, shared) {email,name,role:'admin'|'user',access:{companyId:[tab ids]},active}: access record; no tabs for a company = no access to it. Admins add users and set tabs in the Users tab; firestore.rules require an active users doc and gate writes by tab. Recovery/first admin: `cd scripts && npm install && node set-access.mjs ../serviceAccount.json <email> admin` (the service account key is never committed).
- `meta/settings` {incentiveRate:5}; `meta/months` {list:[...]}; `meta/expenses` {salaries,rent,electricity,fuel,marketing,other}
- Purchasing (Masters and Purchase Orders tabs): `vendors/{id}`, `products/{id}` {sku ('SKU-0001'…, unique; blank = next in series),name,spec,unit,hs,nw,gw,vol,lastPrice,lastPo, optional thickness (mm) and sheet fields altUnit ('SQM'|''),width,height (mm),pcsPerCase; editing name/spec/nw/gw/vol, in the form or by SKU in the bulk upload, copies them onto PO lines, never prices}, `hscodes/{code}` {code,description,bcd,apta (the APTA / SAFTA concession % off BCD; blank = none),sws,igst}, `meta/purchasing` {buyer,markups,chargeHeads,terms}, `purchaseOrders/{id}` {poNumber,date,status:'draft'|'ordered'|'received',vendorId,vendor (copy),rate,freight,insurance,claimApta,containers (number of containers: other charges are per container and multiplied by it; BE interest counted once),charges:[{label,amount}],lines:[{productId,item,spec,qty,price,nw,gw,vol,hs,sp:[override|null]}],terms,piNumber,piDate,ciNumber,ciDate (proforma / commercial invoice no. and date),origin (country of origin on the invoices; blank = China),rates (HS rates frozen at save; drafts use the live master)}. The PO editor prints the supplier PO, the PO for customs, and a proforma and a commercial invoice (`printInvoice` / `exportInvoice`: supplier letterhead, addressed to us; the proforma prices lines FOB with freight, insurance and total CIF at the bottom, the commercial invoice prices them CIF). A vendor with piFormat 'sheet' (vendor master: Proforma invoice layout = Glass sheets) gets the proforma in its own glass-sheet layout instead (`sheetPiParts` / `printSheetPi` / `exportSheetPi`, modelled on Reliance's PI): lines grouped by HS code then description (thickness stripped, from the product master), thickness/length/width/sheets/crates/SQM columns, TOTAL FOB, sea freight, insurance, total value, then the vendor's numbered piTerms ("Label: text" lines, {port} {paymentTerms} {packing} {shipment} {hsCodes} filled from the PO) and bank details plus bankExtra lines.
  Sheet products (altUnit 'SQM' with width × height, e.g. patterned glass): a PO line also carries altUnit,width,height,sqmPerPc,pcsPerCase,cases,pcs (copied when the product is picked); cases × pcsPerCase = pcs and pcs × sqmPerPc = qty, so qty, price, nw/gw, unit cost and selling prices are all per SQM; vol is per pc (total volume = vol × pcs, `lineVol`). Helpers: applySheet / setSheetQty / lineUnit / specWithSheet. Landed-cost maths is the PURCHASING-CALC block in public/index.html; `node scripts/seed-purchasing.mjs serviceAccount.json <company> <workbook.xlsx> [--write]` imports a costing workbook and checks that block against the workbook's numbers.

Firestore runs with a persistent on-device cache (IndexedDB, multi-tab); sign-out clears it. Reopening within ~30 min only bills changed docs; the free plan's 50k reads/day is the limit to watch (every fresh open reads all of a company's orders).

## Business rules
- PO costing (`costing:'gst'` in COMPANIES; `priceHeads` / `costIncGst`): instead of markup selling prices the costing shows cost incl. GST = unit cost × (1 + GST%); SQM lines also per sq ft (÷ 10.764) and per mm (per SQM ÷ product thickness from Masters). Markups stay saved but are hidden.
- Gross sales and total cost include GST. Gross profit = gross − cost. Net profit = gross profit / (1 + GST%/100). GST is 18% or 5% (blank in bulk upload = 0%).
- Incentive (Excel Traders, `incentive:'netSales'` in COMPANIES; `applySalesIncentive` / `renderSalesIncentives`): strictly per salesman per ledger month, no carry over or push back. Net sales = gross sales / (1 + GST%/100); all of the salesman's orders that month count, whatever their status. Month net sales below the threshold (`meta/settings.salesThreshold`, default ₹1,00,000) → every order that month is Not eligible and locked automatically (computed, not stored). Otherwise incentive = rate (`salesRate`, default 1.5%) × (net sales − threshold), shared across the month's orders by net sales.
- Order statuses (`paymentStatus`): 'none' = Pending; 'full' = Issue incentive (issues that order's share, locks); 'noteligible' = Not eligible (locks). A locked order must be unlocked (lock icon) before its status can change.
- (The per-order 5%-of-net-profit incentive with carry over is still in the code for companies without `incentive:'netSales'`.)
- Salesman Y (Yahya) is the owner (`NON_INCENTIVE_REPS` in public/index.html): never eligible. New salesmen are eligible automatically.
- Financial year = Indian FY (1 Apr – 31 Mar).
- True net profit = total net profit − (monthly expenses total × number of months that have orders).
- Bulk upload template columns: Order Ref, Channel, Salesman, Month (YYYY-MM or 1-12), GST %, Gross Sales, Total Cost, Notes. Parse dates from raw Excel serials (no `cellDates`; IST timezone bug).
